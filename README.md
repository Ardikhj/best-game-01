Emberwarden is a top-down arena survival game: hold the last lantern against the dark tide through 10 waves, choosing boons between waves, with synthesized sound and procedural art.

## How it plays

Waves 1–3 teach the ropes — a few wisps, room to learn kiting and the dash. From wave 6 the horde grows denser, tougher and faster than the boons can keep up with, so the last waves are a genuine fight: a good run ends on a sliver of health, and a careless one ends in the dark. No cheap deaths — every hit is telegraphed, the dash gives a moment of safety, and standing still is what gets you killed.

## Skills

Between waves you choose a boon. Most sharpen the lantern; five unlock real skills:

- **Ember Nova** — press **E** to detonate a ring of fire (45 damage, 6 s cooldown) that shoves the horde back. A second pick hits 20 harder.
- **Storm Lantern** — your bolts arc lightning to a nearby foe for half damage. Each pick arcs to one more foe.
- **Ember Feast** — every kill feeds the flame: heal 1 HP per kill.
- **Warden's Aegis** — a shield of dawn that blocks one hit each wave, renewed at every wave's start.
- **Ember Orbit** — a shard of light circles you, cutting anything it touches. Each pick adds a blade.

The dash (Shift) is yours from the start — Gale Step just recharges it faster.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
